Our Roofing Sheets Are Distinctly Different

It is the vision and commitment of Mr Narendra Raval EBS (Guru) to supply building and construction materials at affordable costs to the local Kenyan population. Maisha Mabati Mills began with galvanised and coloured steel roofing sheets.

Our products are largely focused on being durable, functional, strong and cost-effective to enable Kenyans around the country to build better standard homes.
