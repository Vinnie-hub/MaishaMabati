Quality You Can Trust

At Maisha Mabati Mills, quality is at the core of everything we do. Our roofing sheets are manufactured using state-of-the-art technology at our modern alu-zinc plant in Ruiru, ensuring superior strength, durability, and weather resistance.

Amazing Offers

We believe every Kenyan deserves access to strong, reliable roofing without overpaying. That’s why Maisha Mabati offers competitively priced roofing solutions that balance quality and affordability.

Fast & Reliable Delivery
