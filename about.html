Our Story

Building Kenya Since 2009

Maisha Mabati was established in 2009 to meet the growing requirements for quality roofing at affordable pricing. It was the vision and commitment of Mr Narendra Raval EBS (Guru) to supply building and construction materials at affordable costs to the local Kenyan population.

Our products are largely focused on being durable, functional, strong and cost-effective — enabling Kenyans around the country to build better standard homes. We manufacture using state-of-the-art technology at our modern alu-zinc plant in Ruiru.
